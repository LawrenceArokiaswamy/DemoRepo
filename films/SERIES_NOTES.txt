15	## Creative rules (what "world class" means here)
16	- Vertical 1080×1920, the style's frame rate (STYLE.md) or 24 fps. Length 40–50 s. English.
17	- **Hook in the first 1.5 s**: a bold claim, number or question on screen AND in the voice, with motion already happening on frame 1. No logo, no slow open.
18	- **Heart-pounding pacing**: the score builds like a pulse (a low heartbeat-like kick or ostinato under the hook, acceleration into the turn), at least two real silences (or near-silences) right before the turn and before the payoff; the first sound after each silence is a key sound. Vary pace: fast-slow-fast, one breath moment.
19	- **One story with a turn**: setup → tension → twist/payoff → a closing line that loops back to the hook. End with an on-screen "Follow for more" (never promise a "part 2").
20	- Every visible action has a sound. Score in the style's sound palette (STYLE.md §7). Original music only (sampler/synthesis); voice about 10 dB above the music.
21	- Burned-in captions designed in the style, readable on a phone (cap height ≥ 40 px at 1080 wide), never covering faces or key action; hold rules from DIRECTOR.md §7. Also export `<name>.srt`.
22	- Characters: an original cast for THIS film. **No robots.** Faces/staging readable at phone size: subject ≥ 1/3 of frame height at key moments, no tiny far-wide gags.
23	- Keep text on screen short (numbers big, words few).
24	
25	## Voice (Kokoro, `core/tts/tts.py`)
26	- Use the voice named in your film prompt (if the name is unknown, tts.py prints the list; pick the closest match in spirit).
27	- Spell numbers and years out in the TTS text ("nineteen eighty", "seven hundred thousand"), digits in captions. Check every line with `core/tts/asr_check.py`, and double-check doubtful ones with system `python3` + `faster_whisper` model `small.en`. Kokoro mushes short plosive words ("pump" → "thumb"): rephrase until the transcript is right. Leading words after a pause can be clipped: test.
28	
29	## Sound and master
30	- Mix: duck music under voice; voice ≈ 10 dB above the music bed. Master with `sh core/render/mux.sh <video> <mix.wav> <out.mp4> <fps> [grain]` → it must report about −14 LUFS and no peak warning (true peak ≤ −1.2 dB). If it warns, compress/limit your mix bus and remux.
31	- Final checks: `ffmpeg -af ebur128`, `blackdetect`, whisper on the final mp4 (every line must come back right), a contact sheet every ~2 s that you actually look at, 0.2 s strips of key actions. One fix round.
