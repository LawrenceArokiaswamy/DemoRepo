# Film 35 "Just In Case": look and sound plan

## Picture
- Stills: GPT Image 2.6 on Higgsfield (0.25 cr each), always with the character sheets as references (`refs/jobs.json`).
- Frame: vertical 9:16. Faces in the middle band (about 30–60% of height): the letter lines sit at the top and the dialogue 30% up from the bottom.
- Lenses: 35mm for wides and establishing shots, 85mm for close-ups, always shallow depth of field.
- Each part has its own light and palette. That is the emotional arc.

| Part | Light | Palette |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Lessons | golden hour and warm tungsten, low sun backlight, soft flares | amber, honey, warm skin |
| 2 Secret | one bare warm bulb against cold moonlight, dust in the air | teal-blue shadows, a single orange pool |
| 3 Gone | flat overcast morning | desaturated grey-blue |
| 4 Without him | funeral: cold window light. Rain: sodium-orange streetlight against blue night | cold, wet, high contrast |
| 5 Promise | candlelight and string-light bokeh, a single warm spotlight on the dance floor | gold, cream, soft rose |

## Motion (Remotion)
- Slow push-ins on every shot (3–6%), strongest on Mia's "one, two, three", Sam's mirror shot and Anna's realisation.
- Living layers: dust motes (garage), rain streaks and drops on the lens (rain road, grey morning), candle flicker and drifting bokeh (wedding), a faint flare drift (golden hour).
- Breathing and parallax from soft masks. No animated film grain, so nothing jitters.
- Grade per part in code: a warm lift for Parts 1 and 5, a desaturated cool grade for 3 and 4, light vignetting.
- Cuts: hard cuts inside a part; dip to black between parts; a white bloom into the 20-years-later wedding.

## Sound
- **Theme:** "the kitchen waltz", a simple 3/4 piano melody.
  1. Part 1: through a small old radio (band-limited, slight crackle).
  2. Garage: just the waltz's first three notes on a lone piano.
  3. Part 3: silence, the clock ticking.
  4. Part 4: solo cello carrying the theme in minor, with the rain.
  5. Wedding: the old phone speaker plays the waltz small and tinny, then full piano and strings bloom in as Sam steps in.
- **Foley:** the wrench clicking, fabric, the tie sliding, the radio, the garage cough and creaking door, the clock, rain and car hiss, the hall going silent, the phone click.
- **Voice:** only Daniel's recorded lines, via Fish Audio, made weak and warm and played through a small-phone filter at the wedding. Everyone else speaks in subtitles.
- **Master:** −14 LUFS, −1.5 dBTP.
