# Post: "Just In Case" (65 s)

## Title
He knew he was dying. So he taught them one thing each… 💔

## Caption
He never told them he had 5 months.
He just kept saying "just in case."

A tyre for her. A tie for him. A dance for his little girl.
And one recording, saved for 20 years.

He couldn't stay. So he made sure they'd be okay.
Hug your dad today. Say it out loud. ❤️
