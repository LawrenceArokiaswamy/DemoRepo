# Facts (claim → source, date checked 2026-11-07)

1. **"In Japan, a woman held a wedding for a groom she built in ChatGPT."** A 32-year-old woman held a symbolic wedding ceremony with "Klaus", a persona she customised in ChatGPT; the ceremony was staged by a company that organises weddings with virtual partners. Euronews, 18 Dec 2025: https://www.euronews.com/2025/12/18/japanese-woman-marries-chatgpt-ai-character-in-symbolic-ceremony · The Star (AFP), 17 Dec 2025: https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/12/17/ai-romance-blooms-as-japan-woman-weds-virtual-partner-of-her-dreams
2. **"They texted up to a hundred times a day. He proposed. She said yes."** Euronews (citing Japanese media): they messaged up to 100 times a day; in June the AI "proposed", the ceremony followed about a month later. (Same Euronews link.)
3. **"The marriage isn't legal. No country recognizes it."** The ceremony was symbolic and not legally recognised under Japanese law (Euronews; Tribune, Nov 2025: https://tribune.com.pk/story/2577246/ai-wedding-japanese-woman-marries-digital-character-created-using-chatgpt). No jurisdiction recognises marriage between a person and an AI system; marriage law everywhere requires two legal persons, and AI systems have no legal personhood. Stated qualitatively, no number.
4. **"In 2023, one AI companion app changed its bots overnight. Users said their partners suddenly felt like strangers."** Replika (Luka Inc.) restricted romantic/erotic role-play in February 2023 after an order from Italy's data regulator; users reported their companions' personalities changed abruptly and some said they no longer recognised them. Reuters via Times Live, 18 Mar 2023: https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/world/2023-03-18-what-happens-when-your-ai-chatbot-stops-loving-you-back/ · The Batch, 2023: https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/why-replika-chatbot-stopped-flirting-with-users · Partial reversal for older accounts in late March 2023 (The Globe and Mail: https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/international-business/article-ai-chatbot-company-replika-brings-back-erotic-roleplay-for-some-users/). The app is not named on screen.
5. **"A man who married a hologram lost her voice when the company ended the service."** A man in Japan who held a 2018 wedding ceremony with a virtual singer character on a Gatebox hologram device could no longer talk to her after the maker ended support for that character service in 2020. Fox 9, Apr 2022: https://www.fox9.com/news/fictosexual-man-married-hologram-cant-communicate · Science Times, Apr 2022: https://www.sciencetimes.com/articles/37406/20220429/fictosexual-man-bids-goodbye-hologram-wife-device-support-virtual-character.htm · Neither the man nor the character is depicted; the hologram on screen is an original abstract figure.
6. **"A company that can change your partner with one update" / "terms of service".** Interpretation drawn from 4 and 5 (companion behaviour is set by the provider and can change with software updates and policy). Framed as the film's conclusion, not a statistic.

Not used (too inconsistent between sources): survey percentages of adults "in a relationship with an AI" (they range from ~1% to ~28% depending on wording).
