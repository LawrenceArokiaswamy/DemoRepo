## How to read it (if you record it yourself)
- Starts as a fun, slightly gossipy fairy tale (n1–n5), turns eerie at n6, sympathetic for n7–n9, then dry and wry for the ending. Never mock the people in the stories: the joke is on the companies.
- CAPITALS = lean on that word. Leave a clear 0.6 s+ gap after n10: the film drops to dead silence there.
