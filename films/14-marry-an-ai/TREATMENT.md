# Treatment: "What if you married an AI?" (film 14, AI in 60 Seconds)

**Logline.** A real AI wedding looks like a fairy tale, until the camera pulls back and shows where the groom actually lives: on a company's computers.

**Shape (48 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, same 3D neon look as films 12/13: navy night, gold glow, cyan accents, Anton headlines).**

| Beat | Time | Picture | Sound |

**Rules kept.** No robots (the AI is a phone with a light-drawn face), no brand logos (ChatGPT is named only in narration and a text headline, as fact), no real people's likeness (the Japanese couple and the hologram husband are never depicted as them; the hologram is an original abstract figure). Neutral and kind: never mocking the people, the irony is aimed at the ownership problem.
