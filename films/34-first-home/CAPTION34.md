# Post: "Her First Home" (62 s, no voice)

## Title
She packed away her whole life… and never told you. 🤍

## Caption
Before you, she had dreams, a favourite dress, and a name everyone used.
Then she gave you her body, her sleep, hours of pain doctors rate above a broken bone… and herself.

Her body was your first home.
She gave you everything she was. Remind her who she is. ❤️
