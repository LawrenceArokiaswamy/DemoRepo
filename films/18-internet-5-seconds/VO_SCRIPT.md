# VO script: "What if the internet disappeared for 5 seconds?"

New format test (inspired by top-performing faceless science reels): a countdown on screen from frame 1, "you" in a real place, one world, one big transformation, minimal text. 10 lines, about 85 words, film about 34 s. A full second of real silence at zero.

Voice: Fish Audio `s2-pro`, library voice "Explainer", speed 1.08 (n4 at 0.9). Stitched into `take.wav`. Build: `./build.sh --vo take.wav`.

| # | Line | Fish direction |
|---|---|---|
| n1 | What if the internet disappeared for five seconds? | `[intrigued, cinematic, a little hushed] What if the internet DISAPPEARED... [short pause] for FIVE seconds?` |
| n2 | You're in Times Square. It's Friday night. | `[storytelling, vivid] You're in TIMES SQUARE. [short pause] It's Friday night.` |
| n3 | All around you, invisible data is flying. Every phone. Every screen. Every card tap. | `[building, wonder] All around you, [short pause] INVISIBLE data is flying. [short pause] Every phone. [short pause] Every screen. [short pause] Every card tap.` |
| n4 | Three. Two. One. | `[slow, tense countdown] Three. [pause] Two. [pause] One.` |
| n5 | Nearly eight hundred thousand Google searches never happen. | `[hushed, awed] Nearly EIGHT HUNDRED THOUSAND Google searches... [short pause] never happen.` |
| n6 | About twenty million emails freeze in mid-air. | `[hushed, awed] About TWENTY MILLION emails... [short pause] freeze in mid-air.` |
| n7 | Tens of thousands of card payments just hang. | `[hushed, awed] Tens of thousands of card payments... [short pause] just HANG.` |
| n8 | And every phone in this square says the same thing: no connection. | `[quiet, eerie] And every phone in this square says the same thing: [pause] no connection.` |
| n9 | Then, it all comes back. | `[rising, relieved, big] Then... [short pause] it ALL comes back.` |
| n10 | Five seconds. So what would you miss first? Comment below. | `[warm, playful, inviting] Five seconds. [pause] So what would YOU miss first? [short pause] Comment below!` |
