# Treatment: "What if the internet disappeared for 5 seconds?" (film 18)

Format: countdown from frame 1 (mono timer "INTERNET DISAPPEARS IN 0:13.370…"), "you're in Times Square", one continuous world, one transformation, minimal serif text.

- 0–13.5 s: a neon canyon at night, giant billboards, a crowd holding glowing phones, ~64 streams of light arcing from phones and screens to rooftop antennas and the sky. The pulse accelerates; "three, two, one".
- ZERO: one second of true silence. The streams snap and fall as a rain of light, every billboard flickers and dies, phones go grey. "0:00.000 — the internet is gone".
- Frozen five seconds (timer "INTERNET RETURNS IN", time slowed 2.8×): a search bar spinning forever (≈800,000 searches), envelopes hanging in mid-air (≈20,000,000 emails), a card terminal stuck on PROCESSING (10,000s of payments), a phone: "No connection".
- BACK: a burst, the streams regrow from the ground up, billboards blaze back. "CONNECTED".
- End: aerial pull-up; "What would you miss first? comment below 👇".
