# Facts (claim → source, checked 2026-11-07)

1. **"Dozens of people already have chips in their brains, controlling computers just by thinking."** Neuralink reported about 26–27 implanted trial participants by mid-2026 (company updates, June–July 2026, as reported); participants with paralysis control computers by thought. Reported counts vary by source and no official total was found, hence "dozens". Earlier milestone: 12 implanted by Sep 2025 (Fox News). https://www.foxnews.com/health/elon-musk-shares-plan-mass-produce-brain-implants-paralysis-neurological-disease · https://www.eweek.com/news/neuralink-brain-chip-uk-trial-quadriplegia/
2. **"The speed of human thought: about ten bits per second."** Zheng & Meister, "The unbearable slowness of being", Neuron, Dec 2024 (Caltech): human behaviour/thought throughput ≈ 10 bits/s, while sensory systems take in ≈ 10⁹ bits/s. The authors note brain–computer interfaces would still run at that ~10 bits/s limit. https://www.technologynetworks.com/neuroscience/news/caltech-scientists-have-quantified-the-speed-of-human-thought-394395 · https://newatlas.com/biology/slow-speed-human-thought-10-bits-second/
3. **"Slower than an old dial-up modem."** A 56k dial-up modem transfers up to 56,000 bits/s (standard V.90 rate), vastly more than 10 bits/s.
4. **"If everyone asks the same AI, we might all start thinking the same thoughts."** Doshi & Hauser, Science Advances (July 2024): AI ideas made individual stories rated more creative but made the stories more similar to each other (less collective diversity). Framed as "might" in the film.
5. **Everything else** ("every language", "everyone scores a hundred", "smartest person in history") is the film's imagined scenario, not a claim about today. No device can currently install a chatbot in the brain.
