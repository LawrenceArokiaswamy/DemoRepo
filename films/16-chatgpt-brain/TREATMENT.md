# Treatment: "What if ChatGPT was installed in your brain?" (film 16)

Trailer energy, two builds, two silences. Picture: a brain made of 9,000 glowing particles with neurons firing; a gold AI chip slots in on frame 1 and a gold wave spreads through the brain.

| Beat | Picture | Music |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Chip flies in and clunks into the brain, gold wave | Heartbeat + low brass |
| Real today | A screen the brain controls by thought | Pulse |
| Superpowers | Languages and knowledge icons orbit faster and faster | 16th ostinato + toms, riser |
| DROP | Brain blazes gold, shockwave ring | Full drop: sub, drums, brass |
| Twist | Everything dims and greys | Dead silence |
| 10 bits/s | Vertical firehose: a billion bits pour into a funnel, a trickle exits a thin pipe | Mystery pulse, modem chirp |
| Everyone | A particle globe lights up | Second build |
| Tests / same thoughts | Wall of 100% exams; 35 multicoloured brains converge to one colour | Darker minor |
| Silence | "So what makes you special?" | Dead silence |
| Payoff | One brain turns gold with a "?" | Massive hit, warm swell |
| End | "Would you install it?" YES / NO, "tell me in the comments 👇" | Light pulse |
