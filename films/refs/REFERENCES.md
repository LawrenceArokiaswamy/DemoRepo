# Reference reels (for look, motion and storytelling; never copy characters, stories or brands)

## Ref 1: "What your mom did after your first heartbreak" (@tinyplanetlab style, 30 s, part 1)
- Look: real 3D (game-engine/Blender look), simple toy-like people: bob hair as one solid shape, dot eyes,
  tiny features, matte clothes; cosy real-scale interiors (hallway, kitchen, bedroom) with warm window/lamp light, soft shadows, wood floors.
- Motion: real body animation (walking, crouching, setting a plate down, sitting against a door, leaning over a bed, stroking hair). Camera mostly static or slow, eye level and one top-down.
- Structure: hook title on screen ("What your mom did after your first heartbreak", one word in gold), then a counter device
  top-left ("DAYS UNTIL YOU LAUGHED AGAIN 1..5") that advances each beat; one quiet small serif italic subtitle per beat
  ("she didn't knock. she just sat there." / "she left dinner by your door." / "it always came back empty." / "she let you stay home from school.").
- Pacing: cuts at 0.5, 2.8, 5.5, 12, 18, 20.8, 25 s: long holds (4-6 s) on small acts. No voice; music only (-17 LUFS).
- Why it works: tiny specific acts of love, shown not told; the counter makes you wait for the payoff.
