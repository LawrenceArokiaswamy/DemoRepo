# Shot QA checklist (every shot, before anything is sent)

Check at full resolution: crops of the face, both hands and both feet, plus a contact sheet across the whole shot.

## Body and pose
- Hands: palms face a believable way, no twisted or snapped wrists, fingers not inside props or the body; a touch actually touches (no gap, no sinking in).
- Arms: elbows bend the natural way; shoulders not hunched or dislocated; no sleeve stretching or "candy-wrapper" twist.
- Legs and feet: knees bend forward, feet flat on the floor (soles down, not floating or sunk), no crossed or inverted ankles.
- Hips and spine: seat touches the floor or chair; back touches the wall when leaning; no torso bending backwards.
- Head and neck: no extreme neck twist; the eyes look where the head points (at the door, the person or the object).
- Clipping: no hair through the wall or shoulders, no clothes through legs or arms, no limbs through furniture.
- Symmetry: the two sides don't mirror in a robotic way; slight asymmetry in resting poses.

## Motion (watch the clip, not only stills)
- No pops or jumps between frames; eases in and out; nothing slides (feet and seat stay planted).
- Breathing visible but subtle; idle parts are never frozen dead.
- Timing matches the beat: the reach lands on the music, the light goes out on its cue.

## Set and props
- Props at real-world scale next to the character (doors about 2 m, tables about 0.75 m, frames at eye height).
- Nothing floats or intersects; rugs flat; frames level; no gaps where a wall meets the floor or ceiling.
- Every prop belongs in the story space; no distracting object in the foreground.

## Light and render
- Each visible light source actually lights the scene (lamp pools, the door slit glows on the floor).
- Faces readable, not lost in shadow or blown out; eyes catch a highlight.
- No fireflies, noise or denoiser smears; no flicker between frames.
- Focus on the subject's eyes; depth of field consistent through the camera move.

## Frame and text
- Faces and hands never cut awkwardly by the frame edge; headroom sensible for 9:16.
- Subtitles never cover a face or a key action; readable on a phone; the timing reads comfortably.
- Colour and contrast consistent with the reference look (warm practicals, cool shadows).

## Sound
- Every visible action has its sound, in sync to the frame; music under the lines; −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP.
