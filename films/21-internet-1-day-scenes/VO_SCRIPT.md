# VO script: "What if the internet went down for one whole day?"

Countdown-format test #2. A 24-hour clock on screen from frame 1 ("INTERNET STATUS: ONLINE 00:00" → OFFLINE), a real Canadian precedent as the hook, hour-by-hour beats, silence before the bill. 11 lines, film 43 s.

Voice: Fish Audio `s2-pro`, library voice "Explainer", speed 1.1 (n9 0.95). Stitched into `take.wav` (0.85 s silence after n9). Build: `./build.sh --vo take.wav`.

| # | Line | Fish direction |
|---|---|---|
| n1 | What if the internet went down for one whole day? | `[intrigued, cinematic] What if the internet went DOWN... [short pause] for one WHOLE day?` |
| n2 | It already happened. In twenty twenty-two, twelve million Canadians lost their phones and internet. Debit cards stopped working across the country. | `[serious, revealing] It already HAPPENED. [short pause] In twenty twenty-two, [short pause] TWELVE MILLION Canadians lost their phones and internet. [short pause] DEBIT cards stopped working across the country.` |
| n3 | Now imagine the whole world. | `[slower, ominous] Now imagine... [short pause] the WHOLE world.` |
