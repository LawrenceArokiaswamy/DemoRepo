## Illustrative visuals (not data)
- The phone, chat screen and thumb are generic (no brand, no logo; "ChatGPT" is plain text only). The question and the reply ("Technically, yes! 🍕 Just maybe add some fruit.") were written for the film.
- Inside the phone: display layers, circuit board, "main chip" and antenna are stylised, not a specific phone's design. The 1s and 0s are a picture of digital data, not the real encoding of the question.
- The cell tower, city, fibre route and data centre are generic; which data centre serves a request is not claimed.
- The globe: land from Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons, city lights from Natural Earth 1:50m populated places (public domain). The rising sparks stand for questions; their count is not to scale. "Our" question rises from Toronto as an example.
- Threads between the bricks, the pizza-shop map, the guess bars and the clock values are illustrative and labelled where they show numbers.
