# CAPTION: Instagram Reel

**Hook line:** You asked ChatGPT about pizza. Here's the wild trip your question took in about 2 seconds. 🍕⚡

Every day people ask ChatGPT about 2.5 billion questions (per OpenAI). We followed just one:
📱 your words turn into 1s and 0s inside your phone
📡 the antenna fires them to a cell tower, then through a cable of light
🧱 in a data centre, your question becomes numbered "LEGO bricks"
👀 it reads all the words together (pizza + breakfast = food question, not a map!)
✍️ then it writes the answer ONE word at a time, guessing the most likely next word, like "twinkle, twinkle, little… star!"

And it has never once tasted pizza.

💾 Save this for the next time someone says "AI just looks it up".
📤 Send it to the friend who eats cold pizza for breakfast.

Clock and odds are illustrative. Sources: OpenAI help centre ("How ChatGPT and our foundation models are developed"), OpenAI's July 2025 usage figure.
