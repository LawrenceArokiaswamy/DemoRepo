# Facts (claim → source, checked 2026-11-07)

1. **Round 1: "The best American model beats the best Chinese one by just 2.7 percent."** Stanford HAI AI Index 2026: as of March 2026 the top US model led the top Chinese model by 2.7% on Arena (1,503 vs 1,464 points). https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report · coverage: https://thenextweb.com/news/stanford-ai-index-2026-china-us-performance-gap
2. **"Three years ago, that gap was huge."** Same report: in 2023 the gap was 17.5 to 31.6 percentage points across major benchmarks (on screen: "2023: 17–32 PTS").
3. **Round 2: "America has about 72 percent of the world's AI computing power. China? About ten."** Epoch AI, AI chip imports data (published Oct 2026): the US holds 72.2% of the world's AI chip compute delivered since 2022 as of Q2 2026; China 9.6%. https://epoch.ai/topics/geopolitics · coverage: https://cryptobriefing.com/us-dominates-global-ai-compute-share/ (Other Epoch datasets give China ~12–14%; the film uses the latest series and says "about".)
4. **Round 3: "China is adding power plants about six times faster than America."** BloombergNEF projects China will add over 3.4 TW of generation capacity over the next five years, almost six times as much as the US (forecast). Via Al Jazeera, May 2026: https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/5/28/chinas-secret-weapon-in-ai-race-with-us-lots-of-cheap-energy and IEEE ComSoc, Feb 2026: https://techblog.comsoc.org/2026/02/16/china-vs-u-s-generating-power-for-ai-data-centers-as-demand-soars/
5. **Round 4: "In January 2025, a cheap Chinese AI called DeepSeek wiped nearly six hundred billion dollars off Nvidia in one day."** Nvidia lost about $593 billion of market value on Jan 27, 2025 (a record one-day loss for any company), after DeepSeek's low-cost model; shares fell about 17%. Reuters via Business Standard: https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/deepseek-sparks-ai-stock-selloff-nvidia-loses-record-593-bn-in-mcap-125012800095_1.html · Tom's Hardware: https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-loses-usd589-billion-in-market-cap-broad-stock-plunge-triggered-by-deepseek-ai-release (estimates range $560–600B; narration says "nearly six hundred billion", screen shows Reuters' $593B).
6. **"Right now, America leads. But China is catching up, fast and cheap."** Summary of 1–5 plus BCG (2026): US leads on frontier models, talent, capital; China pushes cheaper and open models. https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/us-and-china-ai-strategy-causing-global-ai-divide
