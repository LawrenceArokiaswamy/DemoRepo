# Facts (claim → source, checked 2026-11-08)

This film is **fiction** (an imagined alien invasion). The only real-world claim:

1. **"One million Optimus robots" / on-screen "Optimus: Tesla's humanoid robot, in production since 2026".** Tesla's Q3 2026 shareholder update said Optimus had entered production, with Musk warning the ramp would be slow. Interesting Engineering: https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/tesla-optimus-robot-enters-production · Nobody has a million of them; the number is the film's "what if".
2. The robots on screen are an **original design** (not Tesla's Optimus design, no logos); the aliens, ships, mothership and the "giant" are invented. "Ten kilometers wide" describes the fictional mothership.
