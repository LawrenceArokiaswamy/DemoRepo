# Treatment: "Final Day: 1 million robots vs. aliens" (film 17)

Fiction, war-trailer energy. Red sky, original alien ships, an army of original humanoid robots (640 animated up close, 9,000 more as glowing eyes to the horizon).

Hook: red sky, ships descend → the army's eyes switch on → CHARGE (drop, laser rain) → robots fall, counter 1,000,000 → 180,000 → a 10 km mothership descends → the robots stop, eyes dim, music drains → close-up: "Did they give up?" + dead silence → "NO." every eye flashes → 12,000 particles stream up and assemble a 130 m giant → the aliens back away, the giant raises its fist → beam + mothership bursts, dawn → "WHICH ARMY WINS? 1 GIANT / 1,000,000".
