# voice-scam: treatment

**Logline.** A mother's phone rings with her kid's name on it and a panicked voice begs for money. We take the call apart like forensic evidence, then turn the scalpel on the film itself: every voice you just heard is AI. One fix to remember: a family safe word.

**Format.** 1080x1920, 30 fps, 35.0 s. Style: Visual Investigation (dark evidence desk, greyscale sources, one hot accent #FF4A2E that only points). Overrides from the client brief win over STYLE.md: the camera always moves (push, drift, whip, impact shake), shots last 0.8 to 1.8 s, and the captions are big and kinetic, word by word.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Cold open on the call, then autopsy** (chosen). The call plays live (hook), a hard cut freezes it, and the reconstruction runs source by source: caller ID, waveform, sample length, source clip, transcript, red flags, money. Then a silence, the meta twist and the payoff. It keeps the viewer inside one event and pays off the style's "one clock" idea: the call timer runs in the corner until the money shot.
2. **Countdown of 3 seconds.** Everything is told inside a giant 3-second stopwatch (the sample length). Strong image, but it can't hold 35 s and it buries the safe-word payoff.
3. **Real vs fake split screen.** Two columns, a real call and a cloned call, and the viewer guesses. Interactive, but slow to set up and it reads like a quiz, not a thriller.

Why 1: the first frame already has a human situation (mom, the kid's name, a ringing phone), the twist lands on the film's own voice, and the share is built in (the CTA tells you who to send it to).

## Arc
Setup (0 to 3.3 s): the call. Tension (3.3 to 21.1 s): the autopsy, accelerating pulse, the money. Silence (21.1 to 21.7 s). Turn (21.7 s): "this voice? Also AI." Payoff (27 to 31.9 s): safe word, hang up, call back. CTA (31.9 to 35 s): send this to your parents, follow for more. The end card plays the narrator's waveform with "EVERY VOICE HERE: AI", looping back to the opening's waveform.

## Benchmark (grammar only)
Newsroom visual-investigation explainers and forensic reconstructions: learn the timestamped evidence, the highlight wiping across the decisive words, circles drawn on the evidence and the "known vs claimed" split. Don't take their branding, music or layouts. From thriller trailers: the accelerating pulse, the riser, the hard cut to silence and the hit.

## Cue map
A heartbeat kick (lub-dub) under the hook at 125 BPM. From 3.3 s, four-on-the-floor kick plus a clock tick, accelerating smoothly from 125 to 152 BPM by 21.1 s (beats.json is shared by the score and the picture's glow pulses and beat zoom-bumps). A D minor bass ostinato in 8ths (D - D - B♭ - A roots from 8.9 s), a minor-second cluster drone swelling, and the phone-vibration buzz on the offbeat every other bar as rhythm. Riser 19.6 to 21.1 s (swept noise, rising tone, snare roll), then a hard cut to digital silence 21.10 to 21.70. HIT at 21.70: sub boom, kick, crash and a low chord. 21.7 to 27 s: 128 BPM filtered pulse under a dark pad, a second sub on "AI". The lift at 27.0 s: F - C - Dm - B♭ pads with plucked 8ths at 122 BPM (hopeful but still minor-tinged), and a sub swell on "Follow for more".

| Section | Bed | Foley | Music |

| Autopsy | none | strike scratch, stopwatch ticks, meter ticks, keystrokes for the transcript, highlighter scratches, pen circles, impact thuds and subs on every stamp, counter ticks, dot ticks, whooshes on whips | accelerating pulse, bass, drone, buzz |
| Silence | none | none | none |
| Twist | crash tail | sub on "AI", check marimba notes | hit + dark pulse |
| Payoff | none | ring again, safe-word keystrokes, hang-up click and disconnect beeps, ringback, paper-plane whoosh | lift |
Voice: the scam voice runs through a phone line (band-pass 320 Hz to 3.3 kHz, tanh saturation, faint line hiss). Voices are compressed, music ducked by 9 dB under the voice and balanced about 10 dB under it by RMS.

## Subtitles and type
Kinetic captions, word by word (Whisper word timings from asr_check): narrator in Anton 116 px uppercase, white with a black stroke, the active word on a red box; the cloned voice in Archivo Black 84 px with a red "CLONED VOICE" label and a white box on the active word. Captions sit at y ≈ 1480 (clear of the Instagram UI at the bottom), and layouts stay above y ≈ 1330. Tags and readouts in IBM Plex Mono. The call timer (top right) is the in-picture clock until the money section.

## Not the demo
The style's demo was not opened or used. The structure, opening, signature shot (the film turning the forensic tools on its own narrator), camera, score and ending are all specific to this film.
