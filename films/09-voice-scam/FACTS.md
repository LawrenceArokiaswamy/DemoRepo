## Background (not on screen)
- Canada: the CAFC received more than 112,000 fraud reports and over $704 million in reported losses in 2025, and estimates only 5 to 10% of victims report: https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/news-nouvelles/2026/2026-03-06-eng.htm . I could not find a 2025 CAFC figure just for emergency/grandparent scams, so the film uses the FTC number and labels it U.S.
- McAfee's survey of 7,054 adults in 7 countries: a quarter said they had experienced an AI voice scam or knew someone who had (not used on screen).
