# Find a story

Area (may be empty): $ARGUMENTS

Read `references/formula.md` first. You research and offer; you do NOT make the video here.

## What every option needs
1. A **weird action or object you can show in one picture** (a clock on a ship, chlorine by a door, a cut-up map).
2. **Doubt**: people laughed, refused, banned or ignored it (check that it really happened; "doubted" is fine).
3. **Proof**: a number, a result or a disaster.
4. **Hidden science** a 12-year-old gets in two sentences.
5. **An arguable last line** people will comment on.
Mix the story types across options: laughed-at genius · weird old practice with a real reason · "imagine you…" · a famous person's strange habit.

## Steps
1. Brainstorm 8-10 candidates in the area (no area: spread across medicine, sea, space, food, the body, inventions, animals).
2. Drop: anything in `topics/used.txt` or `stories/`, living private people, gore, politics, and stories that are already
   everywhere on Shorts (search YouTube for the title idea; if the first results are big Shorts on the same story, skip it).
3. Check each survivor with web search: at least 2 reliable sources (museum, university, encyclopedia, major news).
4. Reply with 3-4 numbered options. Each: **title** (under 50 characters), the first line of the script (the weird
   action), the twist and the science in one line each, the arguable last line, and "documented / partly dramatized".
5. Save the options with their sources to `topics/latest_options.md` (overwrite it).
6. End with: **"Pick one: type /make-video 2 (or any number)."**
