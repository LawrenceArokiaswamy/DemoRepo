# Checklist (look at the stills, fix before the full render)

1. **Frame 0** works as a thumbnail: the hero and the hook are visible and big.
2. **Every sentence is on screen**, in order, and each reaction lands on its word (0-3 frames before it).
3. **Faces in frame**: nobody's face cut by the frame edge, hidden behind a post, a sign or another card (unless it's the joke).
4. **The narrow 9:16 frame**: wide groups are staged in depth; the main subject sits near the centre, not at the very top.
5. **No frozen moments**: every character breathes and blinks; something in the world moves.
6. **Signs and text** read the right way round and are big enough to read on a phone.
7. **Continuity**: the same character keeps the same look; props stay where they were left.
8. **Light**: night shots still readable; the payoff is the best-looking shot.
9. **Sound**: the voice is complete and clear, the music sits under it, every effect matches something you see.
10. **The file**: 1080x1920, 30 fps, exactly the voice length plus the tail, with sound. Never call a still or an untested file a finished video.
