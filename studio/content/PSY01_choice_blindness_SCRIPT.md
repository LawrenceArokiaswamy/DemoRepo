**Research** (backlog entry PSY01):
- Johansson, Hall, Sikström & Olsson (2005), *Science* 310:116–119: 120 people, 15 face pairs, swaps detected rarely. Sources report 10–26% depending on how detection is counted, so we say **"fewer than 1 in 5"** (ScienceDaily's summary).
- Hall et al. (2010), *Cognition* 117:54–61: supermarket jam and tea. For cinnamon-apple vs grapefruit jam, **fewer than 20%** noticed (BPS Research Digest).

**The trick on the viewer:** a "test subject" figure stands in the corner of most shots. **His shirt changes colour twice** during the video, slowly, while attention is on the story (change blindness). The ending asks about it. That payoff rewards rewatches.
