**Owner's idea:** "everything in life is scripted, controlled by the top 0.1%".

**Editorial stance:**
- We show the *documented* machinery: algorithms, defaults, shelf placement, wealth concentration.
- We do **not** claim a secret group controls everything (unprovable).
- The twist turns that very idea around, so the video stays credible, monetisable and still unsettling.

**Fact sheet** (all claims attributed and conservative):
1. **YouTube 70%:** chief product officer Neal Mohan at CES, January 2018: 70% of watch time comes from recommendations. Script says "once said". Sources: Tubefilter, Quartz (2018).
2. **Organ donation defaults:** Johnson & Goldstein (2003), *Science* 302:1338–39.
   - Germany (opt-in) 12% vs Austria (opt-out) 99.98% **registered consent**. We say "signed up", not "donated".
   - Caveat: actual donation rates differ less (Arshad et al. 2019).
3. **Slotting and placement fees:** retailers charge manufacturers for shelf placement. Documented industry practice (FTC reports on slotting allowances, 2001/2003).
4. **Few giants own many brands:** Oxfam "Behind the Brands" (2013) on the Big 10 food and beverage companies. We say "many brands trace back to the same few giants", **not** "10 companies control everything".
5. **Wealth:** Federal Reserve Distributional Financial Accounts (reported by the Center for American Progress, 2025): top 1% ≈ 31–31.6% of US wealth, bottom half ≈ 2.5%.
   - Top 0.1% and top 1% shares were at record highs in 2025.
   - Re-check the latest DFA quarter before release.
6. **No unverifiable claims:** no named people, no conspiracy assertions. The 0.1% idea is posed as a question and answered with evidence.
