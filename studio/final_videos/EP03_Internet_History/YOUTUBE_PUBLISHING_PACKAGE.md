## 2. Description (copy-paste)
```
At 9:41 tonight, every search you've ever made goes public. For everyone. All at once.

At first it's funny. Then your boss can see it… your family… strangers. So people stop searching, even when they need it most.

This is a fictional "what if?", but how being watched changes what people look up has been studied. After the 2013 surveillance revelations, researcher Jonathon Penney found that views of privacy-sensitive Wikipedia articles dropped.

If every search became public… would you still dare to ask? Tell us in the comments.

If you're worried about your health or your safety, reaching out for help is always worth it.

Source:
• Jonathon W. Penney, "Chilling Effects: Online Surveillance and Wikipedia Use", Berkeley Technology Law Journal 31 (2016): https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2769645

Fiction: all searches and characters are invented. Animated with original 3D characters.
```
