Sweden built one of the most heavily armed warships of its time… and it sank on its first voyage.

Stockholm, 1628. The royal warship Vasa was built with two full gun decks for 64 cannons. Before she sailed, thirty men ran back and forth across her deck to test her stability. She rolled so badly that the admiral stopped the test. On 10 August 1628 she set sail anyway, in front of the city.

What happened next, and where she is today, is one of the most remarkable stories in naval history.
