This Roman builder is about to ruin his concrete.
He tosses in burning-hot lime.
It hisses...
and leaves little white lumps all through the mix.
Ha!
Sloppy work!
Two thousand years later, his sea wall is still standing...
while the modern concrete beside it has crumbled.
Then a crack opens.
Seawater trickles in...
and the crack begins to seal itself.
Those lumps were tiny pockets of lime.
Water dissolves them, and they refill the crack with fresh stone.
Roman concrete...
heals itself.
