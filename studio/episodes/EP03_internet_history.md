# Episode 3: "What If Everyone Could See Your Entire Internet History?"

**Status**
- Preproduction: **script and storyboard awaiting owner approval** (2026-11-15).
- No paid audio generated; no animatic yet.

**Category:** scientific/social hypothetical (*What If?*).
- The premise is **fiction** and is labelled as such.
- One real-world anchor is fact-checked (§4).

**Ethics guardrails**
- All search queries are invented, generic and harmless (dating nerves, cat facts, fitted sheets).
- No real people and no real private data.
- Nothing humiliating: the joke lands on universal awkwardness, never on a group.
- Serious beats (health, relationships) are handled with empathy, not mockery.

## 1. Story spine
- **Who:** an ordinary guy on a dinner date, plus everyone around him.
- **What:** at 9:41 pm, everyone's entire search history becomes public, everywhere, at once.
- **Why care:** everyone has searched something they wouldn't say out loud.
- **Emotional arc:**
  1. **Humor:** the date reveals.
  2. **Shock:** boss, family, strangers can all see it.
  3. **Consequences:** people stop asking questions, including the important ones. A real-world echo grounds this.
  4. **Twist:** once everyone's history is exposed, shame stops working.
  5. **Open ending:** kinder, or afraid to ask?

## 4. Facts and sources
- **Premise:** fiction (labelled).
- **Line 8, real:** after the June 2013 NSA/PRISM revelations, traffic to 48 privacy-sensitive (terrorism-keyword) Wikipedia articles showed a statistically significant drop and a lasting change in trend.
  - Source: Jonathon W. Penney, *Chilling Effects: Online Surveillance and Wikipedia Use*, 31 Berkeley Tech. L.J. 117 (2016). [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2769645) · [Citizen Lab summary](https://citizenlab.ca/2016/04/jon-penney-publishes-paper-chilling-effects-online-surveillance/).
  - Reported sizes differ (≈20% vs ≈30%), so **no percentage is stated**.
  - The S09 chart is labelled "illustration", not data.

## 5. Asset reuse and production plan
- **Reused (no new building):**
  - restaurant set, lighting preset with ambient occlusion, painted-texture kit;
  - C+ v2 couple and six diners, snap-and-hold helpers, phone rig and screen UI;
  - symbols, sound effects (buzz, ding, café murmur), mix pipeline, captions, thumbnail tool.
- **New (episode-scoped):**
  - **Search-bubble FX:** a UI-card sprite with text, a pop with overshoot, and a bob. Reusable for any "thought or text" beat.
  - **Phone UI states:** sharing cards, empty search bar with typing, chart.
  - **"Next day" and "weeks later" lighting variants:** cooler or warmer settings of the same preset.
  - **Reaction-close-up camera presets.**
- **Material finish (C), applied to the C+ v2 look:**
  - lower rim strength (0.4 → about 0.22);
  - a subtle painted-noise skin and fabric texture to break the plastic gradient;
  - a hair strand-gradient texture;
  - smaller, softer noses on modern builds (radius −25%, less protrusion).
- **Effort:** about 4–5 working days to the final cut, including the animatic, review and audio.
