# PSY04 "The Spotlight Effect": production notes

## v2 (current): rigged character animation (`anim/`)
- **v1 problem:** the owner rejected v1 as "just ppt": still images that only zoomed.
- **v2 approach:** v2 is drawn and animated entirely in code, with an original stick-figure rig.
  - Jointed arms and legs with easing and walk cycles.
  - Blinking, eye darts, expressions, sweat and shake lines.
  - Hand-drawn line "boil" and handwritten write-on headlines.
  - Props: spotlight, door, phone, shirt print, thought bubble, stage and curtains, flashlight.
  - Camera push, pull and shake.
- **Cost:** 0 credits.
- **Build:** `node anim/render.js silent.mp4`, then `node anim/mix.js silent.mp4 out.mp4`.
  - Rendering takes about 20 s.
  - The mix is voice (+0.15 s lead), music ducked under the voice, and 11 cued SFX from the kit's cleared library.
  - Then a two-pass loudness normalisation.
- **Delivery:** `renders/psy04_spotlight_effect.mp4`, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 44.4 s, −14.9 LUFS.
- **Payoff:** the "spotlight" is a flashlight in the hero's own hand. He notices, clicks it off and smiles.
