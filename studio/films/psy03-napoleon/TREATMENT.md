# PSY03 "Napoleon Was Short": treatment

**Format:**
- Halftone Dossier style.
- 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, ≈ 58 s.
- English narration (Kokoro `bm_george`: dry, deadpan report voice), with burned-in caption bars plus a `.srt`.

**Script:** owner-approved 2026-11-18 (`studio/content/PSY03_napoleon_SCRIPT.md`).
**Facts:** `FACTS.md`.

## 1. Three structures
1. **Myth on trial (accusation frame).** "The case of the short emperor": the myth gets a mugshot, exhibits disprove it, then the *viewer's brain* becomes the suspect.
2. **Declassified report.** A redacted page about Napoleon whose black bars lift one by one until "FAMILIAR ≠ TRUE" shows.
3. **Countdown of repetitions.** A counter ticks up every time the claim is repeated across ads, chats and speeches until it "becomes" true.

**Chosen: 1, with a mugshot bookend.**
- The height chart is the perfect native move for a *height* myth.
- Turning the suspect from Napoleon to "YOUR BRAIN" is the twist.
- Ending on the same mugshot with the same line ("Napoleon was short.") stamped **WAS HE?** makes the loop and invites rewatch.

## 2. Logline and arc
**Logline:** a "fact" everyone knows is put on trial, and the real culprit turns out to be the viewer's own brain.

**Arc:**
1. **Setup:** the myth (mugshot).
2. **Turn 1:** he was average (height chart grows).
3. **Turn 2:** the culprit is repetition (1977, 2015).
4. **Test:** the Great Wall.
5. **Ending:** the same line again, now suspicious.

## 3. Benchmark
- **Learn from:** the pacing of tight explainer shorts, one idea per card, every 3–5 s; police-procedural title cards (mugshot, evidence board); 1950s print ads (flat inks, halftone).
- **Don't take:** the library demo's cat, its story, shots or score; any real caricature (we draw our own generic "cartoon print"); real logos or brands.

## 4. Shot list (vertical; camera is a 2D group over the page)
| # | Time | Shot | Framing / move | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | L01 | **Mugshot**: a tiny Napoleon on a height chart, name plate "SUSPECT: N. BONAPARTE"; headline NAPOLEON WAS SHORT.; stamp EVERYONE KNOWS | Locked frontal, flash on the shutter | Hook: a fact the viewer "knows" |
| 2 | L02 | The height chart re-measures: he grows to 169 cm beside an "average man, 1800" outline; giant numeral 5'7"; stamp AVERAGE | Slow push-in 1 → 1.06 | Turn 1, the myth breaks |
| 3 | L03 | **Exhibit A**: a printed "cartoon" sheet with a tiny emperor (our own drawing) plus two rulers: the French *pouce* 2.71 cm vs the English inch 2.54 cm | Pan across the pinned sheet; snap zoom on the rulers | Where the myth came from |
| 4 | L04 | **Mugshot #2**: SUSPECT: YOUR BRAIN; the brain blinks | Hard cut on "So why…", flash | The real culprit |
| 5 | L05 | **Exhibit B**, 1977: one statement card repeated ×3 (weeks 0, 2, 4); a TRUE meter rises each time | Locked, cards slam | The discovery |
| 6 | L06 | Headline ILLUSORY TRUTH EFFECT; card "FAMILIAR = TRUE?"; stamp FALSE | Push-in | Naming it |
| 7 | L07 | **Exhibit C**, 2015: the brain holding a book of correct answers still nods at the repeated lie | Locked, dot density swells | "Knowing doesn't save you" |
| 8 | L08 | **Evidence board**: billboard, podium mic, chat bubbles, each linked by string to the brain; the board shakes | Pan down the board; shake on the last string | It's everywhere |
| 9 | L09 | **Quick test**: Earth limb from orbit; question card; YES / NO; a ticking timer. Music drops to silence | Locked, then a slow zoom | Viewer participation |
| 10 | L10 | Stamp NOT VISIBLE*, with a small source chip "*naked eye, low Earth orbit: NASA"; counter HEARD IT: 100× | Hard cut, shake | Caught again |
| 11 | L11 | The brain's two file drawers: TRUE and FAMILIAR; the card hesitates between them | Pull back | The takeaway |
| 12 | L12 | **Mugshot bookend**: Napoleon at true height; the same headline reprinting; stamp WAS HE? | Locked, then flash; end card | Loop and rewatch |

**Signature shot:** the height-chart re-measure (shot 2). The ruler ticks race up as he grows.
**Transitions:** dot wipe between exhibits; hard cuts at shots 4 and 10 (the big hits).

## 5. Beat sheet
The timeline is built from the measured voice durations in `timeline.json`. Each shot = its line + 0.5–0.9 s air. The silence before shot 10 lasts 1.2 s.

## 6. Cue map
**Tempo:** 100 BPM, 4/4. A pop-print synth toolkit:
- **L01–L03:** kick, square bass, marimba pluck.
- **L04–L07:** pizzicato and snaps.
- **L08:** the full kit, rising.
- **L09:** everything drops; only a clock tick.
- **L10:** stamp boom; groove returns.
- **L11:** pad.
- **L12:** silence → final stamp → bell tag.

Stamps land on beats; the voice never sits under a stamp.
